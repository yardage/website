FAQ

Common questions about Yardage golf yardage books — for clubs and players.

What is the difference between a yardage book and a scorecard?

A yardage book maps each hole: distances, hazards, lines of play and the green. A scorecard is for recording strokes. Yardage designs the former, for clubs.

Does a yardage book replace a rangefinder?

No. A rangefinder gives a one-off distance. The book gives an overview of the hole so you can anticipate the next shots and build a strategy. The two complement each other.

What does a Yardage hole page contain?

Useful distances, hazards, lines of play and a green diagram. The page is designed to be read quickly on the tee and in the fairway.

Can the book be customised with the club logo and sponsors?

Yes. Cover, logo, sponsor page and partner mentions are part of the order. The book remains recognisable as the club’s official yardage book.

What paper are the books printed on?

Eco-friendly paper, with finishes suited to course use and to presentation in the pro shop.

What is the delivery time?

Books are delivered within one month after quote approval, to the club or the pro shop.

What is the minimum order?

150 copies per order. Volume discounts at 500 and 1,000 copies. A digital sample can be prepared on request.

Can the book be updated if the course changes?

Yes. Distances and landmarks live in an updatable base — a new bunker, a rebuilt green, a reshaped fairway — without starting over.

I am a player: can I order a book for my club?

Orders are placed on behalf of the club. Speak to the director, manager or pro shop: they are the ones who request a quote.

How do I get a quote?

Write via the contact form, to contact@yardage.io or +33 5 37 07 97 18. Reply within 48 business hours. Include the club name, number of holes and, if possible, an estimated volume.
