What’s included

The club rate covers the full journey, from design to delivery. You do not need to assemble vendors: Yardage handles the product end to end.

Every order includes customisation of your identity — club logo, sponsor page, partner mentions — so the book clearly carries your brand.

Printing is done on eco-friendly paper, with premium finishes suited to the pro shop and to course use. Delivery is handled through to your club.

On request, we also provide a free digital sample: a simple way to preview the result before committing, or to present the project internally.

Customisation

Club logo, sponsor page, partner mentions — the book carries your organisation’s identity while remaining a playing tool.

We adapt the cover and brand elements to your guidelines without compromising the readability of hole pages. The result should be immediately recognisable as your club’s official yardage book.

How it works

The commercial path is intentionally simple. You keep control of approval; we handle production.

  1. 01

    Contact

    Write to us with the club name, number of holes and, if possible, an estimated volume. We can also prepare a free digital sample.

  2. 02

    Quote

    We review your course and send a clear proposal: pricing, customisation options and delivery terms.

  3. 03

    Approval

    Once the quote is approved, production starts. You validate identity elements (logo, sponsors) so the book matches your image.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Books are delivered within 2 weeks after quote approval — ready for the pro shop, members and competitions.

Who it’s for

The Yardage offer is for organisations that want to provide a professional yardage book without complicating their operations.

  • Golf clubs — showcase the course, enrich the member and visitor experience, strengthen the club’s image.
  • Pro shops — offer a premium, consistent product that is ready to sell.
  • Professionals — support their players with a clear, reliable preparation tool.

Whether you order a single copy to discover the product, or volume to equip your season, the pricing stays transparent — with discounts from 500 and 1,000 copies.
