The editions

Three editions, one standard of care.

Every book is produced for one specific course. Choose the edition that suits your players; we discuss it together when preparing the quote.

STANDARD

The reference book for the course.

  • Useful distances, hazards and lines of play
  • Green diagram
  • Club cover and identity

SLOPE

For players who want to read the slopes of the green.

  • Everything in the STANDARD edition
  • Green slope reading
  • To prepare the approach and the putt

TOUR

For competition and the most demanding players.

  • Designed for club competitions
  • Content defined with you in the quote
  • Club cover and identity

How it works

A simple path, from request to delivery.

You keep control of approval; we handle production.

  1. 01

    Contact

    The club writes to us with the club name, number of holes and, if possible, an estimated volume — from 150 copies.

  2. 02

    Quote

    We review your course and send a clear proposal: pricing, customisation options and delivery terms.

  3. 03

    Approval

    Once the quote is approved, production starts. You validate identity elements (logo, sponsors) so the book matches your image.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Books are delivered within 1 month after quote approval — ready for the pro shop, members and competitions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order? +

150 copies per order. A digital sample can be prepared on request.

What is the delivery time? +

Books are delivered within one month after quote approval, to the club or the pro shop.

Can the book be customised with the club logo and sponsors? +

Yes. Cover, logo, sponsor page and partner mentions are part of the order. The book remains recognisable as the club’s official yardage book.

What paper are the books printed on? +

Eco-friendly paper, with finishes suited to course use and to presentation in the pro shop.
