Personal data

Privacy

How we handle data collected through the website.

Controller

For any question about your personal data, contact us at contact@yardage.io. Billing for Yardage orders is handled by PLATEFORME IO.

Data collected

The contact form collects the information you provide (name, email, club, volume, message) in order to respond to your quote request or digital sample request.

Purpose

This data is used only for commercial follow-up and relationships with golf clubs and professionals interested in Yardage. It is not sold to third parties.

Processors

The contact form is sent through Formspree. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Fonts are installed on the site: no third-party service is called to display them. Please consult those providers’ privacy policies for more information.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by writing to contact@yardage.io.
